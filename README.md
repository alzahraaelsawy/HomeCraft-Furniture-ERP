# HomeCraft Furniture

**Odoo ERP Implementation Project**

## 1. Project Overview

HomeCraft Furniture is a commercial furniture business that purchases furniture products from suppliers and sells them to customers, while also offering related services such as installation and customization.

This project demonstrates how Odoo ERP can help organize and integrate the company’s business operations, financial activities, inventory management, and branch performance analysis.

## 2. Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Organize furniture products into clear categories.
- Manage furniture products and related services.
- Connect sales, purchasing, inventory, and accounting operations.
- Improve stock tracking and product availability.
- Analyze financial performance across different branches.
- Monitor budgets and compare planned amounts with actual results.

## 3. Product Catalogue

The project includes the following product categories:

- Living Room
- Bedroom
- Dining Room
- Office
- Kitchen
- Outdoor
- Home Accessories
- Furniture Services

The catalogue includes product references, descriptions, costs, sales prices, and other relevant information.

## 4. Furniture Services

In addition to physical furniture products, HomeCraft offers related services, including:

- Installation
- Customization
- Upholstery
- Dismantling
- Cleaning

These services can be included in the same quotation or sales order as furniture products.

## 5. Odoo Business Workflow

The proposed operational workflow includes:

1. Customer Request
2. Quotation
3. Sales Order
4. Inventory and Delivery
5. Customer Invoice
6. Financial and Management Reporting

## 6. Inventory and Accounting

The project addresses inventory and accounting configuration through:

- Differentiating stockable products from service products.
- Managing receipts, delivery orders, and internal transfers.
- Configuring product categories and inventory valuation.
- Connecting inventory movements with the relevant accounting records.

## 7. Branch Analysis

The project uses Analytic Accounting to analyze revenue and costs across two branches:

- Cairo Branch
- Main Showroom – Assiut

This helps evaluate branch performance and supports better management decisions.

## 8. Budgeting and Performance Monitoring

The budgeting process focuses on:

- Defining budget periods and expected amounts.
- Linking budgets to the relevant analytic accounts.
- Comparing planned amounts with actual financial activities.
- Reviewing and analyzing budget variances.

## 9. Expected Business Benefits

Using Odoo ERP is expected to help HomeCraft Furniture achieve:

- Better organization of business data.
- Improved inventory visibility.
- More consistent product pricing.
- Integrated sales and service management.
- Better branch-level financial analysis.
- More effective budget monitoring and planning.
